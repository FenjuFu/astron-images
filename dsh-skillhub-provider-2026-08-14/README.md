# DSH × SkillHub Provider visual assets

Bilingual visual assets for the proposed `@iflytek/dsh-skillhub` integration campaign, produced on 2026-08-14. The story frames are 1080×1920; the animated provider maps are square overlays used inside those vertical frames.

## Contents

- `01`–`08`: Chinese vertical story frames.
- `09`–`16`: English vertical story frames.
- `17`–`18`: animated Chinese and English provider maps.
- `19`–`20`: final-video contact sheets used for visual QA.

The story is grounded in the provider contract exposed by [DeepSeek Harness](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness), the enterprise registry capabilities of [SkillHub](https://github.com/iflytek/skillhub), and the ready-made content supply in [iFly-Skills](https://github.com/iflytek/iFly-Skills).

`@iflytek/dsh-skillhub` is a package proposal, not an existing published package. The diagrams describe a recommended adapter architecture and controlled-refresh flow; they do not claim that the integration has already shipped.

No credential, private registry address, or production token appears in these assets.
